Pancit Canton


Description

Pancit Canton is a popular Filipino noodle dish made with stir-fried egg noodles, vegetables, and meat or seafood. It is commonly served during birthdays and celebrations because noodles symbolize long life and good health.

Ingredients

  • 500g pancit canton noodles
  • 200g chicken breast (sliced)
  • 100g shrimp (optional)
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon oyster sauce
  • 3 cloves garlic (minced)
  • 1 onion (sliced)
  • 1 carrot (sliced)
  • 1 cup cabbage (chopped)
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • 2 cups chicken broth or water

Step-by-Step Cooking Instructions

  1. Sauté Aromatics - Heat oil in a pan and sauté garlic and onion until fragrant.
  2. Cook the Meat - Add sliced chicken and cook until lightly browned.
  3. Add Shrimp - Add shrimp and cook for about 2 minutes.
  4. Add Broth and Sauce - Pour in chicken broth, soy sauce, and oyster sauce.
  5. Add the Noodles - Add pancit canton noodles and let them absorb the liquid.
  6. Add Vegetables - Add carrots and cabbage and mix well.
  7. Serve - Once noodles are cooked and sauce is absorbed, serve hot.

Beef Kare-Kare


Description

Beef Kare-Kare is a traditional Filipino stew known for its rich peanut-based sauce. It is commonly cooked with beef shank or oxtail along with vegetables such as eggplant and string beans. The dish is thick, savory, and usually served with shrimp paste (bagoong) on the side.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg beef shank or oxtail
  • 1/2 cup peanut butter
  • 2 tablespoons ground toasted rice
  • 1 onion (chopped)
  • 4 cloves garlic (minced)
  • 1 eggplant (sliced)
  • 1 bunch string beans
  • 1 banana blossom (optional)
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • 6 cups water
  • Shrimp paste (bagoong) for serving

Step-by-Step Cooking Instructions

  1. Boil the Beef - In a pot, boil the beef in water until tender (about 1.5–2 hours).
  2. Sauté Aromatics - In another pot, heat oil and sauté garlic and onion until fragrant.
  3. Add Beef and Broth - Add the cooked beef and some broth to the pot.
  4. Add Peanut Sauce - Mix peanut butter with broth and pour it into the pot.
  5. Thicken the Sauce - Add ground toasted rice and simmer until the sauce thickens.
  6. Add Vegetables - Add eggplant, string beans, and banana blossom.
  7. Serve - Serve hot with shrimp paste (bagoong) and steamed rice.

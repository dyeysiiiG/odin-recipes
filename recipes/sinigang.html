Pork Sinigang


Description

Pork Sinigang is a famous Filipino sour soup known for its refreshing and comforting taste. It is cooked by simmering pork with vegetables in a tamarind-based broth. The sour and savory flavor makes it one of the most loved Filipino comfort foods, especially during rainy days.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg pork belly (cut into cubes)
  • 6 cups water
  • 1 pack sinigang mix
  • 1 onion (quartered)
  • 2 tomatoes (quartered)
  • 1 radish (sliced)
  • 1 eggplant (sliced)
  • 1 bunch kangkong
  • 2 long green chili peppers
  • Fish sauce or salt to taste

Step-by-Step Cooking Instructions

  1. Boil the Pork - In a pot, combine pork and water. Bring to a boil and remove the scum that floats on top.
  2. Add Aromatics - Add onions and tomatoes. Let it simmer for about 30–40 minutes until the pork becomes tender.
  3. Add the Sour Base - Add the sinigang mix and stir well to combine.
  4. Add Vegetables - Add radish and eggplant. Cook for about 5–7 minutes.
  5. Add Remaining Ingredients - Add kangkong and green chili peppers.
  6. Season the Soup - Add fish sauce or salt according to your taste.
  7. Serve - Turn off the heat and serve hot with steamed rice.
